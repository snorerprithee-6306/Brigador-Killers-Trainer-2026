## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Brigador Killers**. This trainer provides configurable advantages for on-foot missions, vehicle operations, firefights, arsenal management, salvage, and fabrication. It is designed for the single-player Early Access campaign and supports both human-scale combat and armored deployments.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the campaign, test weapons and combat rigs, experiment with vehicle builds, reduce repeated resource management, or replay missions with relaxed difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Brigador Killers supports operations on foot and in vehicles. Use the trainer after entering active gameplay, then move normally through the isometric environment. Unlimited health protects the player during exposed movement, while unlimited vehicle integrity helps maintain control when crossing hostile areas.

### Tools & Inventory

The game lets players build and steal a private arsenal, find different combat rigs, salvage vehicles for parts, fabricate new tools of destruction, and mount weapons to new creations. Activate inventory-related options before spending salvage or modifying equipment. Reopen the arsenal or fabrication interface if a value does not update immediately.

### World & Environment

Missions take place across the SNC homeworld of Mar Nosso. Players can travel through detailed sprite-based environments, disrupt corporate control, fight local forces, and pursue Brigador targets. Use gameplay options for exploration and experimentation, but deactivate combat enhancements when mission objectives require normal damage or resource behavior.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat switches between human-scale firefights and vehicle engagements involving sedans, tanks, and bipedal mechs. Activate unlimited ammunition or no reload before engaging enemies. One-hit kills is best used for testing weapons, clearing difficult encounters, or quickly replaying completed missions.
